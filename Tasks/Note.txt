A B O U T

This is a program to practice Spaced Revision Technique
A schedule is created for spaced revisions once you enter the things you studied through - Add Topic
Status Update is to update when you complete the scheduled revisions with reviews to how easy or hard it was
Reviews:
    - 1 for easy    -> no updates in the schedule
    - 2 for medium  -> next revision schedules for this topic are made a little closer
    - 3 for hard    -> schedules made further close
Shift Schedule is used in case you have something planned in coming days and you wouldn't be able to revise
In case of missed scheduled revisions, when the app is opened, it automatically shifts all the missed ones to today

I N S T R U C T I O N S

After installing docker, logging in with your account and pulling this repo, do the following:
1. Start Docker 
    - Open docker windows(other os) app 
    - Must be running in the background
2. Open CMD in the folder where all the pulled code-files are present 
    - Go to file location, click on the path, enter cmd and press Enter
3. Enter: docker compose build
    - THIS IS ONE TIME ONLY
    - Wait for it to build, it may take some time
    - Close this CMD after confirmation
4. Enter ./run-tasks.bat(CMD) or ./run-tasks.sh(Bash)
